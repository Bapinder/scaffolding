# What the other scaffolding apps actually offer
Reviewed 20 August 2026. Sources: vendor feature pages (Avontus, SMART Scaffolder, CloudScaff, Footprint WFM, Scaffolding Software/iScaf, Scaffcalc, ScaffPlan, Scafflinq, Brand Access) and 2026 UK job-management comparisons.

The market splits into four camps. Almost nobody sits in more than two of them.

## 1. Design & estimating only — draw it, count it, quote it
| Product | What you get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Scaffcalc** | Browser 3D design, structural/FEM load calcs, automatic bill of materials, PDF proposals. Tiers: Quotation → Project Planning → Engineering → Enterprise (unlimited users from Pro) | Quote-based |
| **ScaffPlan** | Automated 3D in SketchUp or Tekla, gear lists, Trimble Connect collaboration | £2,600/user/yr (SketchUp), £3,550 (Tekla) |
| **Scaffmax** | SketchUp plugin — draw the surface, it places the scaffold | Not published |
| **NTI Scaffplanner** | Revit/BIM plugin, material lists, transport weights | Custom quote |
| **LayPLAN** | Layher-system erectors, 2D assembly sketches + 3D viewer | Via myLayher |
| **iScaf 11** | Plan/elevation/3D drawings with automatic component counting | Contact sales |

No job tracking, no sign-off, no calendar. These are drawing tools.

## 2. Full ERP — yard, rental, invoicing
| Product | What you get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Avontus Quantify** | Serialized + non-serialized inventory, equipment forecasting, multi-project/multi-branch visibility, estimating by piece/weight/unit rate, automated invoicing (advance, arrears, FATA, off-rent and continuation periods), credit-hold rules, QuickBooks/Xero/Sage via REST API, mobile equipment requests, QR scanning, interactive site maps, inspections | ~$199–$349 per 28 days per plan + start-up fees |
| **iRent / iQuote** (Scaffolding Software) | Quote CRM with unlimited project stages and hire/labour/transport/sundries budgets; multi-yard inventory, delivery & return dockets, utilisation reporting, cross-hire, inter-yard transfers; progress-claim invoicing by area and stage, day labour, variations | Contact sales |
| **CloudScaff** | Inventory (levels, movements, multi-yard, bulk ops, orders), site management (scaffold register, geolocation, site instructions, tasks), customer portal, forms + e-signing, chat, notifications, QR, reporting, accounting integrations, unlimited users, free trial | Not published |

This is where the money and the complexity are. Aimed at firms with yards full of stock.

## 3. Compliance-first — inspections, handovers, certificates
| Product | What you get |
|---|---|
| **SMART Scaffolder** (Estimator / Manager / Inspector / Handover / Compliance) | Take-offs and quotes; project + workforce tracking, **variations and extra hire**; mobile inspections with defect tracking, scheduling and audit trail; digital handover certs with **electronic signatures**; TG20:21 compliance checks and drawings; BIM/Revit imports; Xero/Sage/QuickBooks. Field apps record build/dismantle progress with notes, photos, audio and video |
| **Footprint WFM** | Handover certificates, weekly inspections, harness/lanyard checks, dismantle notices, timesheets; offline mobile; geo-tagged and timestamped submissions; automatic document delivery to the customer; custom forms (50+ field types); live KPI dashboards |
| **Scafflinq** | QR "scafftags" on each scaffold, inspection app, built-in handover certs |
| **Brand Inspection Manager** | Whole lifecycle: handover → inspection → off-hire → decommission, documents shared with the customer |
| **Doka Hi-Vis / CORE EHS ScafEdge** | Client-side: electronic scaffold requests, permits to erect/modify/dismantle, tagging, live site view |

## 4. Generic trade job managers (what many scaffolders actually use)
**Tradify** £34–£44/user/month · **Joblogic** from £45/licence/month · ServiceM8, Simpro, Commusoft, Fergus, BigChange. Quoting, scheduling, invoicing, payments — solid admin, but no scaffold vocabulary at all: no up/down state, no inspection clock, no handover certificate, no verification.

## What they offer that we don't (yet)

Ranked by how much it matters for a small firm:

1. **Quote → job → invoice in one chain.** Every commercial product does this; it's the main reason firms pay. Ours starts at "job exists."
2. **Variations and extra hire on the job.** SMART Manager and iRent both call this out by name. It's the most under-billed thing in scaffolding.
3. **Timesheets / labour hours per job** (Footprint, Tradify) — without them you can't see margin per job, only revenue.
4. **Defect tracking on inspections** with a rectify-by date (SMART Inspector).
5. **E-signature on handover** (SMART Handover, CloudScaff) — a signed cert is worth much more than a recorded one.
6. **Customer portal / auto-send documents** (CloudScaff, Footprint, Brand) — kills the "can you email me the handover" calls.
7. **Materials and stock**, multi-yard, delivery/return dockets (all three ERPs). Only worth it if you own a lot of kit.
8. **Offline mobile with geo-tag and timestamp** (Footprint) — real value on sites with no signal.
9. **Accounting integration** (Xero/Sage/QuickBooks) — universal expectation.
10. **PPE / harness / vehicle checks** (Footprint).

## What almost nobody offers — keep these, they're the wedge
- **Two-person verification** with the doer blocked from signing off their own work. The others record who did it; they don't require a second name.
- **Questions threaded on the job.** Not present in any product reviewed — this happens on WhatsApp everywhere.
- **Hire money live on the job card** (days up × weekly rate).
- **No accounts.** One link, pick your name. Every product above needs user provisioning.
- **Zero cost.** Five people on Tradify is ~£2,000/year; on Joblogic ~£2,700.

## Recommended build order (photos and QR parked as agreed)
1. **Variations & extra hire** on a job — description, price, date, who approved it. Recovers real money for a day's work.
2. **Quote → job**: line items (erect price, weekly hire, adaptations), printable quote, "accepted" turns it into a booked job; completion produces an invoice summary.
3. **Timesheets on tasks** (hours per person per job) → labour vs job value = **margin per job**.
4. **Inspection defects** — fail an inspection, record the defect and a rectify-by date, which becomes a task.
5. **Off-hire chase list** — scaffolds standing past their planned dismantle, with a ready-to-send client message.
6. **Client pack** — printable/read-only handover + inspection history for the main contractor.
7. **Weekly print pack** — what's up, what's due, what's overdue, one page for the Monday meeting.
8. **CSV export shaped for Xero/QuickBooks** rather than a full integration.
9. **Rough materials count per job** (tube, board, fittings) and a yard total — the cheap 80% of inventory.
